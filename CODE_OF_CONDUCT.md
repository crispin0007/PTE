# Code of conduct

openpte is built by volunteers for learners on every budget. We want everyone to feel welcome, whatever their
English level, country, background or experience.

**Expected:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give feedback on the work rather than the person, and
help newcomers, especially people contributing in their second or third language.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual content, doxxing, spam, or pressuring anyone to
share copied exam material.

**Reporting:** open a private report to the maintainers through the repository's Security tab, or contact a
maintainer directly. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove content and block people who
break these rules.
